OPERATIONS MANUAL — WATCH OFFICER

FIRST WATCH

New players: BEGIN WATCH starts a short guided first watch (skippable). Tips appear the first time something new happens; switch them off in SETTINGS. Hover any number for an explanation.

CAMERA

Mouse wheel / Q E: zoom. Left-drag, middle-drag or WASD / arrows: pan. F: focus selection. 0 / Home: whole section.

TIME

SPACE pause · 1 = 1× · 2 = 2× · 3 = 4×. Auto-pause stops the clock on critical events. Orders can be given while paused.

CREW

Click a crew member (or their row) to select. Right-click a room for orders: move, repair, extinguish, seal breach, restore power, investigate, decontaminate, operate reactor, man security, rest, treat. Right-click another crew member to treat, observe or escort. TAB cycles crew.

Frightened, exhausted or grieving crew may refuse dangerous orders. You can INSIST — it costs stress and morale.

DOORS

Click a door: OPEN / CLOSE / LOCK / SEAL. Remote control needs power on either side. Without power, a crew member must crank it by hand (select crew, right-click the door). LOCKs need the SECURITY bus.

ROOMS

Click a room for atmosphere, damage, doors and controls. DISPATCH sends the best available crew. EMERGENCY SEAL clamps doors and vents shut. VENT ATMOSPHERE dumps the room to space: it kills fire, and anything breathing.

POWER

The reactor rarely covers everything. Shut buses down to choose what goes dark. Life support makes air. Cryo keeps 2,400 colonists alive. Security runs cameras and locks. Sensors run motion trackers and biomonitors.

INFORMATION

A room you cannot see needs power + a working camera + the SECURITY bus. Otherwise it goes dark: you get only life signs and motion (if SENSORS are on), and what your crew's helmet lamps show.

MEDICAL

Select a crew member: MEDICAL SCAN (quick, med bay), BLOOD TEST (quarantine lab, 2 supplies, better), QUESTION, OBSERVE, QUARANTINE. Tests are not perfect.
